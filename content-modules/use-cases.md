# SchemaSafe — Use Cases

### SMB websites
- **Pain:** Marketing tags fire before consent.
- **How SchemaSafe helps:** Scan URL; get violation detail + fixes.
### Agencies
- **Pain:** Many client sites need recurring checks.
- **How SchemaSafe helps:** Team: multi-site / white-label oriented plans.
### CCPA opt-out
- **Pain:** US traffic needs Do Not Sell / Share links.
- **How SchemaSafe helps:** Flag missing opt-out patterns.
### Continuous monitoring
- **Pain:** Drift after campaigns.
- **How SchemaSafe helps:** Higher tiers support more frequent scans.

ref: GDPR Art. 7 (consent) · CCPA / CPRA overview (California AG)
